THE VAYU FLEET

Three purpose-built unmanned aerial systems engineered from the ground up for surveillance, heavy-lift delivery, and maritime rescue operations.

CALLSIGN // VAYU-UAV-01 — SURVEILLANCE & RECONNAISSANCE

DRISHTI

CONFIGURATION: Quadcopter Platform
endurance 28 Minutes
range 6.5 km Telemetry
maxSpeed 65 km/h
mtow 3.2 kg

High-Endurance Surveillance Quadcopter fitted with 3-Axis Stabilized Gimbal Camera

Drishti is Team VAYU's frontline autonomous reconnaissance quadcopter, purpose-engineered for persistent aerial surveillance, perimeter inspection, and target tracking. Equipped with an onboard neural vision accelerator and a 3-axis stabilized optical sensor.

MISSION CAPABILITIES

Autonomous Waypoint Navigation & Geofence Patrol
Real-Time Object Detection & Deep Learning Target Tracking
Encrypted 1080p 60fps Low-Latency Video Transmission
Sub-Second Target Lock with Auto Gimbal Horizon Stabilization
Automated Fail-Safe Return-to-Launch (RTL) on Radio Loss
Precision Optical Flow Hovering in GPS-Degraded Environments

COMPONENTS USED & HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS

Airframe Custom 450mm 3K Twill Carbon Fiber Quad Frame with CNC Billet Clamps
Flight Controller Cube Orange+ with Triple Redundant IMUs & ADS-B Receiver
Propulsion 4x 2216 880KV High-Efficiency Brushless Motors with Carbon Propellers
Electronic Speed Controls 4x 40A BLHeli_32 DShot600 Micro ESCs with Active Freewheeling
Camera & Gimbal SIYI A8 Mini 4K 8MP 6X Zoom Sensor with 3-Axis Brushless Gimbal
Telemetry & GCS Herelink HD 2.4GHz Integrated Transmission & Dual Antenna System
Power System 4S 6200mAh 75C High-Density Lithium Polymer Battery Pack
CALLSIGN // VAYU-UAV-02 — HEAVY LIFT & AID DELIVERY

RAKSHAK

CONFIGURATION: Hexacopter Platform
payload 2.0 kg Payload
endurance 22 Minutes (Full Load)
range 8.0 km Telemetry
mtow 8.4 kg

Heavy-Lift Autonomous Payload Delivery Hexacopter with 2.0 kg Cargo Capacity

Rakshak is designed for emergency relief, disaster aid, and autonomous medical supply delivery. Powered by a heavy-lift six-rotor propulsion configuration, it features an automated servo release latch that delivers payloads precisely to targeted coordinate zones.

MISSION CAPABILITIES

2.0 kg Active Cargo Capacity with Centered Gravity Alignment
High-Precision Servo-Actuated Automated Drop Bay
Hexacopter Rotor-Loss Fail-Safe (Controlled Landing on 5 Motors)
LiDAR-Based Real-Time Ground Proximity & Terrain Following
Dual RTK-GPS Centimeter-Accuracy Landing Guidance
Wind-Resistant Operation in Gusts Exceeding 25 Knots

COMPONENTS USED & HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS

Airframe 850mm Heavy-Duty Carbon Fiber Hexa Frame with Folding Arm Locks
Flight Controller Pixhawk 6C Autopilot paired with Dual F9P High-Precision RTK GPS
Propulsion 6x T-Motor U5 400KV Heavy-Lift Brushless Motors with 16-inch Carbon Props
Electronic Speed Controls 6x 60A T-Motor Flame High-Voltage ESCs (IP55 Weatherproof)
Drop Mechanism Custom Aircraft-Grade Aluminum CNC Quick-Release Latch Mechanism
Altimetry Sensor Benewake TFmini-S Micro Solid-State LiDAR Rangefinder (12m)
Power System 6S 16000mAh Solid-State High-Capacity LiPo Battery with Smart BMS
CALLSIGN // VAYU-UAV-03 — MARITIME & WATER RESCUE

SAGAR

CONFIGURATION: Sealed Marine Quadcopter
protection IP66 Water Resistant
endurance 25 Minutes
dropCapacity Inflatable Buoy
mtow 3.8 kg

All-Weather Water Rescue Drone with Life-Buoy Deployment & Marine Telemetry

Sagar is Team VAYU's dedicated maritime rescue platform. Equipped with water-resistant composite seals, floatation landing skids, and an emergency pneumatic life-buoy deployer, Sagar is built to locate stranded swimmers or flood victims and drop lifesaving equipment.

MISSION CAPABILITIES

Water-Resistant Sealed Monocoque Fuselage with Floatable Skid Struts
Pneumatic Emergency Self-Inflatable Life-Buoy Drop System
Ultra-High Output 2000-Lumen Marine Searchlight for Night Missions
Starlight Low-Light Night-Vision FPV Sensor for Zero-Light Water Rescue
Automated Marine Distress RF Beacon Deployment
Anti-Corrosion Marine-Grade Hardware and Coated Electronics

COMPONENTS USED & HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS

Airframe Hydrophobic Coated Carbon Fiber Monocoque with Gasket-Sealed Canopy
Flight Controller Conformal-Coated Pixhawk 6C Autopilot with Pressure-Sealed Baro Chamber
Propulsion 4x IP67 Waterproof Brushless Motors with Salt-Resistant Ceramic Bearings
Electronic Speed Controls 4x 50A Waterproof ESCs with Internal Thermal Cutoff Sensors
Rescue Payload Pneumatically Deployed Water-Activated Self-Inflating Life Buoy Module
Search Optics RunCam Night Eagle Starlight Camera with 2000-Lumen High-CRI Spot LED
Power System 4S 8000mAh Marine-Sealed High-Capacity LiPo Battery
